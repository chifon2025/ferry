# 心燈概念圖產生紀錄

日期：2026-09-07。
工具：內建 image_gen；原創 ui-mockup，未使用參考圖或外部音樂。
圖檔：heartlight-concept-v1.png。
用途：新美術與三個直式互動狀態的設計審閱。並非可操作介面。

## 最終提示詞

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: original visual redesign concept board for a mobile portrait contemplative game named 渡. Create ONE polished landscape presentation image with THREE equal tall portrait mobile app screens side by side, each approximately 390:844 aspect ratio. Cream background outside the screens, small discreet labels above the screens: "停一停", "按住", "鬆開". These are three states of ONE screen, not different levels. No physical phones or perspective tilt.
Art direction: exquisite tactile miniature garden, sculptural layered paper and matte ceramic mixed with hand-painted gouache textures, soft rounded organic forms, sophisticated storybook world, inviting to adults. Rich depth and charming intricate foliage at edges balanced with broad clear central space. Sage green, warm ivory, dusty peach, honey amber, tiny muted terracotta accents. Soft early morning light, gentle cinematic shadows, restrained bloom. Avoid generic neon meditation app, sci-fi, spiritual religious icons, realism, flat vector, dark gloom.
Identical garden geometry across all three screens: distant soft terraced hills in peach mist in upper third; a delicate single branching leafy tree curves over scene from upper left; a tiny softly lit arched garden opening in middle distance; foreground cream stone rounded platform among sculptural moss, grasses and a few little flowers at edges. NO river, boats, characters, quests, inventory, progression, collectibles, numbers, badges, rewards or bottom navigation.
Main interactive object: ONE warm luminous translucent amber heart-light, a soft organic oval like a hand-sized smooth glowing pebble, on the rounded platform at 66% screen height, large enough to comfortably press with a thumb. It is the single clear focal object, occupying 26% of screen width. Fine warm grey silk filaments loop gently around the light. No literal heart icon. No hard knot and no chains.
LEFT state at rest: soft full oval light loosely encircled with two delicate silk loops; environment already beautiful and complete.
CENTER state being held: same light gently compressed inward, silk loops drawn slightly closer, overall garden unchanged and equally beautiful. A very subtle translucent thumb contact ring on light, no actual hand. Warm color stays.
RIGHT state just released: same light softly expands to natural oval; loose silk filaments open gracefully to the sides, a few translucent featherlike warm luminous wisps fade gently, environment unchanged and no magical new treasure or flowers as rewards. Quiet satisfying tactile release.
Mobile UI: exact Traditional Chinese title "渡" at upper left of each screen, tiny simple three-line menu icon upper right. Beautiful clean readable typography, very few words. One short line near upper-middle: left "心裡的事，先放在這裡"; center "想抓住的，想推開的"; right "都可以，先鬆一點". Bottom at safe distance from edge, small text: left "按住，再鬆開"; center "隨時都能鬆手"; right "帶著這份鬆，回到生活". No other text. Main instruction comfortably readable on phone, honest shippable UI hierarchy. No watermark. This board should make the press and release motion understandable and showcase a completely redesigned warm visually rich yet extremely simple single-screen phone game.
```

## 檢視結果

- 三幅皆為直式畫面，同一場景中的停留、按住、鬆開。
- 繁體中文字可辨識；主畫面三句與底部提示符合提示詞。
- 按住的光團有收縮，鬆開後光仍保留；沒有計分或新增獎勵物。
- 成圖光團比初始規格更大，適合作為觸控焦點；實作仍以真實手機拇指測試調整大小。
- 鬆開畫面的羽狀光絲僅作短暫動畫參考，實作需降低亮度、快速淡出，避免遮擋主提示。
- 實作文字將採系統字型並獨立疊放，圖中文字不能取代可存取的 HTML 文字。
